# I Tried CodeBuddy's Free Hy3 for One Day — It's Behind the Domestic Models

> TRAE killed its free tier, so I spent a day on CodeBuddy's free Hy3 model doing light doc tasks. The honest verdict: Hy3 lags behind the domestic models I've used, and long tasks cost too much rework. Here's the day's record.

## Background / Problem

TRAE recently changed its pricing and removed the free tier. Since I knew CodeBuddy's Hy3 was free, I spent today putting it through some light document-work tasks — partly to see if the free tier is good enough, and partly to evaluate Hy3 itself.

## What happened in the day

Today I tried CodeBuddy + Hy3 on some simple document-flow tasks I'd described earlier.

The feeling: Hy3 is still behind the domestic models I've used.

My usual habit is long tasks — I write the requirements clearly and hand them over in one pass. This time the output had too many problems: it didn't fully understand the original content, and it didn't fully understand my needs. I spent a lot of time reworking and fixing.

> Hy3 has a long way to go, and right now it's visibly behind.

## Plan for tomorrow

Tomorrow I'll try some simple coding tasks and see how it does.

But one thing is already clear: I won't hand it long tasks. Otherwise I can't control the quality myself. Long tasks stay on the models with steadier comprehension.

## Context: my tool rotation

A few months ago I tried most domestic tools and free tiers. CodeBuddy itself is pretty good — Tencent's UX is solid, and it also plugs in third-party models. Last month I subscribed to TRAE for a deep month; this month it's Qoder; next month I plan to subscribe to CodeBuddy for a proper month-long test. The domestic models and tools are genuinely competing and growing.

## Mistakes, tradeoffs, alternatives

- The trap: a "free" model stops being free once you're fixing its mistakes for an hour. Rework cost is the real price.
- Why not just use it for everything: long-task comprehension is where stronger (often paid) models earn their keep.
- When I would NOT recommend Hy3 yet: any multi-step task where you can't easily verify and control the output.

## Conclusion & further reading

- Hy3 runs simple doc tasks but lags behind the domestic models I've used.
- Long-task rework is high; I won't trust it with long tasks yet.
- Tomorrow: simple coding test. Next month: a deeper CodeBuddy subscription trial.
